Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01/04/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Expense Tracker app ("we", "our", or "app") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information depending on features you use:

  • Account Information: If you sign in using Google, we may access basic profile details such as your email address.
  • Financial Data: Expenses, income, and categories entered by you.
  • Device & Usage Data: Limited data may be collected by third-party services like ads providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core app features (expense tracking, reports, etc.)
  • To enable backup, restore, and sync across devices
  • To support optional account-based features (Google Sign-In)
  • To display advertisements

3. Data Storage & Proof / Receipt Images

  • By default, your financial data and transaction records are stored locally on your device.
  • Proof / Receipt Images: Any proof images, receipts, or bills you attach to transactions are stored strictly on your local device storage for privacy. For privacy protection, we do not store, upload, or transfer your attached images to any cloud servers. If you clear app data or uninstall the app, local images may be permanently deleted.
  • If you use backup or sync features, your text transaction metadata may be securely stored in cloud services, while images remain offline on your device.
  • Shared or synced data between users/devices is automatically deleted after successful delivery, except for backup data.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In (for authentication)
  • Cloud services (for backup and sync functionality)
  • Advertising services (such as Google AdMob)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared as required to provide app functionality (such as syncing between devices).

6. Data Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • Backup data is retained until you remove it.
  • Temporary sync data is deleted automatically after delivery.

7. Your Control

You can:

  • Use the app without signing in (limited features)
  • Delete your data anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the app

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
